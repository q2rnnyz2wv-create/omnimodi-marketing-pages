How a Student uses OmniModi

Three steps to smarter revision

1

Pick a subject and a mode

Choose from 13 GCSE subjects and switch between question practice, learning notes, AI Teacher, games or a short Rest.

→
2

Read, answer, ask

Read notes in the mode you prefer — summary or detailed, plain or technical. Answer questions with instant explanations. Ask the AI Teacher when something is unclear.

→
3

Track and keep the streak

Weak spots are flagged automatically. Earn XP, build streaks and level up. Rest between sessions to stay fresh.

The five Student modules

These are the modules available to a Student today. Everything is designed for revision on a phone.

🧠

Question practice

Retrieval practice across 13 GCSE subjects with instant, worked explanations. The single most effective revision technique in the cognitive-science literature.

📖

Learning notes

Every topic in four variants — summary or detailed, plain-language or technical — so a Student can read it the way that lands for them.

💬

AI Teacher

A patient, subject-aware chat tutor. Ask for a re-explanation, a worked example, or a nudge in the right direction — without giving the answer away.

🎮

Games

Interleaved, timed and streak-driven drills that turn revision into short, focused loops. Weak spots feed straight back into the next round.

🌿

Rest

Short, calm breaks between sessions. Guided breathing and low-stimulation moments to reset attention before the next question.

🏆

Progress and streaks

XP, streaks and levels run underneath every module. Weak spots are logged automatically so revision keeps compounding.

Meet the tutor frogs

Every Student picks the tutor personality that suits how they learn. Nine frogs, nine teaching manners — the frog you choose actually changes how the AI Teacher talks to you, not just which picture is shown.

Pond
Friendly and laid-back

Talks to you like a friend who happens to be good at this.

Forest
Calm and grounded

Never rushes you. Explains things one step at a time.

Coral
Gentle and encouraging

Notices what you did well, especially when you find it hard.

Sunset
Playful and cheerful

Keeps things light and uses examples from real life.

Summer Lotus
Reflective and thoughtful

Asks what you think first, then works through it with you.

Winter Frost
Clear and analytical

Breaks problems into logical parts and compares them side by side.

Sapphire
Direct and confident

Gets straight to the point with no waffle.

Royal Gold
Formal and precise

Uses proper terminology. Good if you want exam-ready wording.

Cosmic
Curious and imaginative

Links what you are learning to bigger ideas and what-ifs.

Hover a frog to see them lift.
